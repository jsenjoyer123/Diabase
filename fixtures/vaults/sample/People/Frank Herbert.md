# Frank Herbert

American science-fiction writer, best known for the Dune Chronicles. Worked as a journalist and
photographer before *Dune* was rejected by around twenty publishers and finally printed by Chilton, a
company that mostly published car repair manuals.

## In the vault

- [[Dune]] — five stars, the gold standard.
- [[Dune Messiah]] — the corrective.
- [[Children of Dune]] — currently reading.

## Notes

He researched sand dune stabilisation in Oregon for a magazine article that never got finished; the
ecology of Arrakis came out of that.
