## Why I want to read it

The follow-up to [[Thinking, Fast and Slow]], co-written with Olivier Sibony and Cass Sunstein. Bias is
systematic error; noise is the random scatter in judgments that should be identical (two judges, same
case, wildly different sentences). Sounds directly relevant to code review and hiring at work.

## Before I start

- Borrow from the library rather than buy.
- Skim the reviews that say it's repetitive; decide whether to read it selectively.
