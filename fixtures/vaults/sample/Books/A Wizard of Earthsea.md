## Review

A short book that feels enormous. Ged's pride lets a shadow loose into the world and he spends the rest
of the story chasing it, then being chased by it. The magic system (knowing the true name of a thing)
has been borrowed by half the fantasy I grew up with, but it's cleanest here.

Read it in two long evenings. Good palate cleanser after [[The Pragmatic Programmer]].

## Quotes

> To light a candle is to cast a shadow.

> Only in silence the word, only in dark the light, only in dying life.
