## Review

I put this off for years because everyone told me it was "slow at the start", and they were right,
but the slowness is the point. [[Frank Herbert]] spends the first hundred pages teaching you how to
read Arrakis: the politics, the water discipline, the way every dinner party is a knife fight. By the
time Paul and Jessica are in the desert, I cared about every litre of water.

What surprised me most is how little of it is about heroics. Paul's arc is a warning, not a power
fantasy, which makes [[Dune Messiah]] feel inevitable rather than like a twist.

## Quotes

> I must not fear. Fear is the mind-killer.

> The mystery of life isn't a problem to solve, but a reality to experience.

## Things I want to remember

- The appendices on ecology are worth reading on their own.
- Compare the Fremen's relationship to water with the Anarresti in [[The Dispossessed]].
- Next in the series: [[Children of Dune]] (after Messiah).
