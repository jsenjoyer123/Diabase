## Review

An envoy, Genly Ai, is sent alone to the ice planet Gethen, whose people have no fixed sex. I expected
a thought experiment and got one of the best survival stories I have read: the crossing of the Gobrin
glacier with Estraven is eighty pages I could not put down.

[[Ursula K. Le Guin]] never lectures. The ideas about gender, loyalty and patriotism are all carried
by two people learning to trust each other in a tent.

## Quotes

> Light is the left hand of darkness, and darkness the right hand of light.

> It is good to have an end to journey toward; but it is the journey that matters, in the end.
