## Review

One good idea (make the habit obvious, attractive, easy and satisfying; identity before outcomes) spread
across three hundred pages of anecdotes. The habit-stacking trick does work: "after I pour my coffee, I
write the daily note" is why the [[2026-10-01]] streak exists.

Two stars because I'd recommend the summary over the book.

## Quotes

> You do not rise to the level of your goals. You fall to the level of your systems.
