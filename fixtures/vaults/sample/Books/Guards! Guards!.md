## Review

The first City Watch book. A secret society of very stupid men summons a dragon to install a puppet
king, and the only people standing in the way are Captain Vimes, who is drunk, and Carrot, who is
six-foot-six and was raised by dwarfs. It's funny on every page and furious underneath.

[[Terry Pratchett]] does the thing I love most: the jokes are load-bearing. You laugh at the footnote
and then realise it was the thesis.
