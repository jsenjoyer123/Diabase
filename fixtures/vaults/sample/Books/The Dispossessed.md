## Review

Shevek, a physicist from the anarchist moon Anarres, travels to the capitalist planet Urras. The
chapters alternate between his past and present, and the two timelines meet in the middle like a
proof. Le Guin is fair to both worlds, which is why it hurts.

I gave it four and a half stars, which my rating system doesn't really allow. It loses half a star for
a slow stretch on Urras; it would be five otherwise.

## Quotes

> You cannot buy the revolution. You cannot make the revolution. You can only be the revolution.

> True journey is return.
