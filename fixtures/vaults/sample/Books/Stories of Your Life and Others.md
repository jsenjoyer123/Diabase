## Review

Eight stories, no filler. Each one takes a single idea seriously and follows it all the way down:
what if the Tower of Babel actually reached the vault of heaven? What if learning an alien language
rewired how you experience time? [[Ted Chiang]] writes like an engineer who happens to be a poet.

## Favourites

1. "Story of Your Life" — the basis for *Arrival*, and better than the film.
2. "Tower of Babylon"
3. "Hell Is the Absence of God" — still thinking about it weeks later.
