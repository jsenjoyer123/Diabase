## Review

Fun, sweeping and overconfident. The chapters on the cognitive revolution and shared fictions (money,
nations, companies as stories we all agree to believe) are genuinely clarifying. The later chapters
speculate far more than they admit, and I started fact-checking claims instead of reading.

Three stars: great conversation starter, not a book to cite.
