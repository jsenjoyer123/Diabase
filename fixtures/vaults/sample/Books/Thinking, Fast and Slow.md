## Review

System 1 is fast, intuitive and confidently wrong; System 2 is slow, effortful and lazy. That frame
alone was worth the book. [[Daniel Kahneman]] walks through anchoring, availability, loss aversion and
the planning fallacy with experiments you can run on yourself.

It took me almost two months because I kept stopping to argue with it. Some of the priming studies in
part one have not replicated well, which Kahneman himself later acknowledged.

## Quotes

> Nothing in life is as important as you think it is, while you are thinking about it.

> A reliable way to make people believe in falsehoods is frequent repetition.
