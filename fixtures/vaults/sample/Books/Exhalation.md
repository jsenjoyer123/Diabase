## Review

The second collection is gentler than [[Stories of Your Life and Others]] and a little more uneven.
"The Lifecycle of Software Objects" is the standout: a novella about raising digital pets over a decade,
which turns out to be about labour, obsolescence and love. The title story made me cry on a tram.

Finished it late on a Saturday night; I logged the exact time because I wanted to remember the moment.

## Quotes

> Every desire I've ever had, every thought I've ever had, is the playing out of the same pattern of
> pressure.
