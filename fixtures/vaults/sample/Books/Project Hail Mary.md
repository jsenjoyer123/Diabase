# Project Hail Mary — review

A schoolteacher wakes up alone on a spaceship with no memory of how he got there, and slowly works out
that he is humanity's last chance against a microbe that is dimming the Sun. Pure competence porn,
in the best way: every chapter is a science problem solved with a whiteboard and a bad joke.

And then Rocky shows up, and it becomes a book about friendship. I did not expect to care this much
about a spider-shaped engineer who talks in musical chords.

## Quotes

> I penetrated the outer cell membrane with a nanosyringe. "Did it pop?" "No it did not pop."
